F&L Checkout Rates (Shopify app)

This section applies specifically to F&L Checkout Rates, our Shopify application, and describes what that app collects and how that information is handled. The automatic collection described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy does not apply to the app. F&L Checkout Rates sets no cookies and collects no device identifiers, geo-location, IP address or web log data.

What the app collects from a merchant's store

  • The store's myshopify.com domain and the access token Shopify issues at installation.
  • The merchant's ShipPrimus username, and their ShipPrimus password in encrypted form. The password is never stored in plain text and is never displayed back.
  • Freight dimensions the merchant supplies for their own product variants: length, width, height, weight, packaging type, freight class, commodity description, hazardous-material flag, and an optional origin address.
  • The merchant's shipping-price settings: pricing mode, markup, free shipping threshold, and freight cap.
  • The merchant's default origin address.

What the app collects at checkout

  • When a shopper requests shipping rates, Shopify sends the app a rate request. We log that request and the rates returned, in order to diagnose pricing problems and support merchants.
  • A rate request happens before an order exists. It carries no customer name, no email address, no phone number, no payment information and no order number, because Shopify has not created an order at that point and does not send them.
  • A rate request does contain a delivery address, because freight cannot be priced without knowing where it is going. It also contains the contents of the cart being priced: item names, quantities, weights, variant identifiers, prices and order totals. The delivery address and the cart contents are the only shopper information the app receives or stores, and neither identifies the shopper.
  • Rate request logs are automatically deleted within 91 days of being created. Deletion runs on a daily schedule against a 90-day cutoff.

What the app does not do

  • No data is sold.
  • No merchant or shopper data is shared with advertisers.
  • No shopper data is used for marketing.
  • No shopper profiles are built, and none can be, because a rate request carries no identifier that would let one shopper be recognized across visits.
  • The app sets no cookies and collects no device or location data.

Who the app shares data with

To return a rate, the app sends the shipment's origin, destination, weight, dimensions, packaging and accessorial requirements to ShipPrimus, the freight rating platform, under the merchant's own account. ShipPrimus then requests rates from the LTL carriers enabled on that account. This is the only third party the app transmits shipment data to.

Uninstalling and deletion

When a merchant uninstalls the app, the store's Shopify session is deleted and the callback the app uses to serve rates is revoked, so the app can no longer quote for that store. Uninstalling does not by itself erase the merchant's stored data. ShipPrimus credentials, freight dimensions, shop settings and rate request logs are retained, because an uninstall is frequently a reinstall in progress and erasing them at that moment would cost the merchant a password re-entry every time.

Shopify sends a shop redaction request approximately 48 hours after uninstall. On receiving it, all remaining data for that store is deleted, including the stored ShipPrimus credentials, the freight dimensions, the shop settings, and that store's rate request logs.

Requests about a specific customer's data

Shopify allows a store owner to request the data an app holds about one of their customers. The app holds no customer-identified data. Rate requests occur before an order exists and carry no customer identifier, email address or order number, so no stored record can be associated with a named customer and there is nothing to return. Rate request logs contain a delivery address and the contents of the cart being priced, and nothing that identifies the shopper. They are deleted within 91 days.
